# Publishing checklist

The first public release is ready only after the repository owner confirms:

- [ ] I directed or authorized the creation of the exported assets.
- [ ] I own, or have sufficient authority over, the inputs used for these
      exported outputs.
- [ ] I intend to apply CC0 1.0 Universal to my rights in the exported assets,
      original scripts, manifests, and documentation.
- [ ] I understand that CC0 cannot waive another person's rights, trademarks,
      patents, privacy/publicity rights, or non-waivable moral rights.
- [ ] `validate_release.py` returns `PASS` on the exact commit to be published.

Checking this file is a release-governance record, not a substitute for legal
advice.
